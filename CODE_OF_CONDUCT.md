# Code of Conduct

Brainrot TCG is a small game with a public Hall of Fame. Be kind, keep names and issues clean, and
assume the people on the other side are other players.

## What we expect

- Use display names, issue text and comments that you would be fine seeing next to a stranger's pull.
- No harassment, slurs, hate, sexual content, personal information about others, or spam.
- Don't try to game or flood the Hall of Fame intake.

## Reporting a problem or requesting removal

Open an issue at <https://github.com/evanlin23/BrainrotTCG/issues/new/choose> and pick "Conduct
report or removal request". GitHub Issues is the only
public contact: there is no email address. Use it for a conduct report, a name you want reviewed, an
entry you want removed from the Hall of Fame, or a takedown request for art or other content
([docs/POLICIES.md](docs/POLICIES.md), "Content and IP"). Say which entry or item, and don't paste
anything private into a public issue.

## What a maintainer can do

A maintainer can hide a Hall of Fame entry from the published list by adding the `rejected` label to
its issue; the next deploy leaves it out. Signed proofs are permanent and copies on relays or
elsewhere can't be recalled (see [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), "Display names").
They can also close or remove issues and comments that break this code.
